    Everything on the island (and yes, all of it works)

    NEW

    Everything breaks

    Rockets blow holes in walls, tank shells and C4 knock them down, a plane flown into a tower can bring the whole thing down. Your diamond pickaxe breaks anything for material; build with it, block by block.

    80

    The Needle

    An 80-floor megatower. Ride the updraft in its core to the top, loot rare guns on every floor, jump out of a window and glide off.

    NEW

    Tanks and motorbikes

    Tanks drive through walls and their cannon flattens houses. Motorbikes are the fastest thing on wheels, with room for a friend on the back.

    NEW

    Knocked, not out

    100 health and 100 shield, fights that are over fast. In duos and squads you get knocked first: crawl to cover and your team picks you up.

    Cars, helis, planes

    67 vehicles on every island. Run people over, drive-by from the window, fly a helicopter with a nose gun or a fighter plane with bombs. Your squad rides with you.

    NEW

    Anti-air warfare

    Stinger missiles lock onto aircraft and chase them. Rocket launchers wreck cars. Planes crash in flames.

    12 guns, 3 upgrade stars

    From pistol to the one-shot Heavy Sniper and the SCAR. Upgrade benches and kits add up to +66% damage.

    9 perks

    C4 (a full health bar, your own too, shields save you), molotovs, shockwaves, smoke, launch pads, instant forts, upgrade kits and the atomic bomb.

    Supply drops

    A balloon crate falls into every new circle: a guaranteed legendary, C4 or a nuke, full shields.

    2x

    A huge island

    800 by 800 m: an 80-floor megatower, a 30-floor skyscraper, two castles, a desert town and a pyramid, a snowy peak and a ski lodge, mountains to climb, a port with a ship.

    Solo, duos, squads

    Party codes put friends on one team. Spectate, free camera, anti-cheat that hides enemies behind walls.

    $

    Winner takes the pot, every hour

    Trading fees fill the King Pig. Every match scores points: a win, a top-10 finish, every kill. Whoever has the most points when the hour closes takes the whole pot, in SOL.
